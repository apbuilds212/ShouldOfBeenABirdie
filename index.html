Master Leaderboard

Ranked by score relative to par — lowest wins. When the live board is on (● Live up top), each group's designated scorekeeper updates this in real time and every phone sees it. Otherwise it shows cards saved or entered on this phone.

Add / Update a Group's Card

Got a text from a group with their final score? Enter it here to add them to the board.

Honesty Check

Every group's row shows who's keeping score ("kept by …") so there's a name behind every number. Scores are entered hole-by-hole with automatic par labeling, or self-reported. If a number looks suspicious (looking at you, group that "shot 64")... that's between you and the 19th hole. ⛳
